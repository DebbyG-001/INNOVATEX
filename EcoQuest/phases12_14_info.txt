PHASE 12 — ONBOARDING AND PLACEMENT

==================================================



Do NOT destroy the current onboarding system.



Preserve the existing placement/segmentation logic.



The onboarding result must:



- belong to the correct user

- persist in PostgreSQL

- survive refresh

- survive logout/login

- be available to the dashboard



If goals can be created during onboarding, those goals must be real database records.



==================================================

PHASE 13 — GAMIFICATION

==================================================



Gamification must be driven by actual backend actions.



Do NOT let the frontend simply say:



"User completed mission."



Instead:



successful backend action

↓

domain event/action

↓

gamification service

↓

evaluate missions

↓

award XP

↓

update streaks/badges where appropriate



Examples:



add money

create goal

contribute to goal

create bill

pay bill

make transfer



All should be capable of triggering appropriate gamification.



Prevent duplicate XP/rewards when requests are retried.



==================================================

PHASE 14 — MISSIONS

==================================================



Missions should be dynamic.



Do not show fake completed missions.



Examples:



Add ₦10,000



Create your first goal



Contribute ₦5,000



Pay an electricity bill



Make your first transfer



Mission progress must come from actual stored activity.
